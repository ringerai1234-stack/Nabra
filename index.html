01 The dialect problem 01 / 09

Most voice AI hears Arabic. Almost none of it hears Masri.

Cairo · the caller never switches to Fusha, and never has to.

Models trained on Modern Standard Arabic fall apart on the street language your customers actually use on the phone. Nabra treats Egyptian Arabic as a first-class target, not a fallback.

None of these are broken Arabic. This is how business gets done on the phone in Cairo — and an agent that pauses, mistranslates, or answers in Fusha loses the caller in the first ten seconds.

    Proving the number is yours

    Caller ID alone proves nothing — it can be forged. So we ask for two independent things: that you can place a call from the line, and that you can answer one on it.

    01Call us from the lineWe show a six-digit code. Ring our verification number from the line you are claiming and key the code in. We match the code against the caller ID.
    02We call you backA second code is read out to whoever answers that line. You enter it here. Possession of the number now has to be genuine, not spoofed.
    03A live test callOnce forwarding or your SIP route is set, we place a real call and confirm it lands on your agent before anything goes live.
    04Paperwork, only for portingMoving a number to us needs a signed authorisation and a recent operator invoice in your company name. Forwarding and SIP need neither.

    A number can be active on one account at a time. Attempts are rate-limited and locked after repeated failures, every verification is logged, and we re-verify annually or whenever account ownership changes.
